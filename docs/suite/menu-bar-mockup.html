Orbit's menu bar panel

The panel that opens from Orbit's menu bar icon, with one tab per section. Each section gets a mockup here as we design it. Device and monitor names are placeholders until Orbit reads your real ones.

Layout: tabs here, side rail in Settings

The menu bar panel keeps the tabs across the top, so it opens fast and stays small. Orbit's full Settings window gets the side rail, with room for deeper options and a Features page where you turn each section on or off.
